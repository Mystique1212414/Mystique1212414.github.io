How Beat2Tube Works

A streamlined four-step pipeline from local studio audio files to a live TikTok post without leaving your desktop workstation.

Step 01

Prepare Audio & Cover Art

Select your mastered WAV or MP3 track along with your cover image inside the desktop interface. Beat2Tube locally renders a TikTok-ready vertical video file.

Step 02

Connect TikTok via OAuth

Sign in securely using TikTok Login Kit. Beat2Tube uses OAuth 2.0 authorization so your TikTok password is never shared with or stored by the application.

Step 03

Configure Post Settings

Write your video caption, choose your privacy level (Public, Friends, or Private), and set interaction preferences directly inside Beat2Tube prior to upload.

Step 04

Direct Post to Profile

Transmit the rendered video straight to your authorized TikTok profile using the Content Posting API Direct Post endpoint—no manual mobile transfer required.

Key Features for Music Creators

Engineered to eliminate repetitive manual steps while maintaining full creator control over every upload.

High-Quality Local Video Rendering

Combine lossless or compressed audio with artwork into crisp 1080x1920 H.264/AAC MP4 videos optimized for TikTok playback standards, processed entirely on your computer.

Native TikTok Direct Post

Publish finished music videos directly to your TikTok feed via the official Content Posting API without needing to send files to your phone or finish drafts manually in the mobile app.

Transparent Creator Controls

Preview your connected TikTok display name, configure audience visibility, and toggle comments, duets, and stitches before every submission in compliance with TikTok UX guidelines.

Official TikTok API Integration

Beat2Tube integrates strictly with official TikTok for Developers endpoints. All media rendering happens locally on the user's machine, and files are transmitted directly over encrypted HTTPS connections to TikTok servers upon explicit user action.

Users can revoke Beat2Tube's access at any time from their TikTok account settings under Security & Permissions → Manage App Permissions.

  • user.info.basic Displays your connected TikTok profile name and avatar inside the desktop app so you can verify the target account before posting.
  • video.publish Enables Direct Post functionality via the Content Posting API to publish creator-approved videos directly to your TikTok profile.
